Level 1 — AI Fundamentals

Default: rotation −2.5° to +2° (varied per card), white face, 3px ink border, radius 14, hard shadow 8×8. Hover: straighten, lift −6px, shadow turns yellow 12×14. Training page: rotation 0 (straight) — warmth stays, silliness dials down. Inner images: 2px ink border, radius 8.
Motion: each card is placed — drops in, one small overshoot, rests (--ease-settle). Tilted cards then breathe ±0.3° over 8–12s; straight cards stay still. Rotation, delay, settle and drift durations are seeded per card, so no two siblings match and no stagger is even. Reduced motion: cards appear in place, no drift.
