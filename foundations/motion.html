Annotation

Written in left to right, after the content it comments on lands.

Always

  • One hero motion moment per page; everything else is quiet
  • Motion ends at rest — entries play once, then stop
  • Jitter every repeated element: rotation, delay, duration (seeded, stable per element)
  • Respect prefers-reduced-motion: final state, no drift
  • Animate transform, opacity, clip-path, stroke only

Never

  • Fade-up-on-scroll on every section
  • Even stagger (0, 100, 200, 300ms…) — the most common machine tell
  • Looping bounces, pulses, shimmer, floating blobs, parallax
  • Default ease / ease-in-out — use the three curves
  • Motion on body text, or anything moving while being read

Jitter recipe: each [data-jitter] element gets a seeded random from its index → sets --r (−2.5° to +2°), --delay (±80ms around its slot), --d-settle (540–680ms), --d-drift (8–12s).
