GC Artifacts — how the brand speaks

Always
  • Punchy, action-first: “Learn. Build. Make it happen.”
  • Lifelong learner — learning is evolutionary
  • “Vibe-coded — just curiosity and a good brief”
  • Logo: plain “GC Artifacts”, no decoration
Never
  • Self-deprecating headlines (“I can’t code”)
  • “Zero coding background” as prominent copy
  • Apologetic framing of any kind
  • Highlight boxes on the logo

The proof of “anyone can build with AI” should feel empowering, not apologetic. East & West identity (Hong Kong · Singapore · Shenzhen) stays present in copy.
